  <h1>Privacy Policy for IBG‑SGDgames</h1>
  <p><strong>Last updated:</strong> 11 September 2026</p>

  <p>Thank you for playing games developed under IBG‑SGDgames.</p>
  <p>This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use IBG‑SGDgames titles.</p>

  <h2>1. Overview</h2>
  <p>
    IBG‑SGDgames titles covered by this policy are designed to be fully offline and do not display any ads.
    The games do not connect to online services while you play and do not use in‑game analytics or tracking.
  </p>

  <h2>2. Age requirement</h2>
  <p>
    IBG‑SGDgames titles are intended for users aged 13 and over. While the games do not contain content that is
    unsuitable for younger audiences, they are not specifically designed for children.
  </p>
  <p>
    We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13
    has provided personal information to us outside of the games (for example, by email), please contact us so we
    can remove it.
  </p>

  <h2>3. Information collected</h2>
  <p>
    IBG‑SGDgames titles are offline and do not collect or transmit personal data from within the game.
  </p>
  <p>Specifically:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>No online accounts:</strong> The games do not require you to create an account or log in.</li>
    <li><strong>No network communication:</strong> The games do not send gameplay data, device information, or identifiers to external servers.</li>
    <li><strong>No analytics:</strong> The games do not use analytics SDKs or tracking tools.</li>
    <li><strong>No ads:</strong> The games do not integrate advertising services and do not show ads.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    Any data stored by the game (such as local save files, settings, or progress) remains on your device and is
    controlled by you. You can delete this data by uninstalling the game or clearing its local storage.
  </p>

  <h2>4. Third‑party services</h2>
  <p>
    IBG‑SGDgames titles covered by this policy do not integrate third‑party advertising, analytics, or social
    media SDKs.
  </p>
  <p>
    If you obtained the game through a platform (for example, a store or launcher), that platform may have its own
    privacy policy and may collect information independently of the game. Please refer to the platform’s privacy
    policy for details.
  </p>

  <h2>5. Data retention</h2>
  <p>
    Because IBG‑SGDgames titles do not transmit gameplay data to us, we do not retain in‑game personal data.
  </p>
  <p>
    Any information you choose to share with us outside of the games (for example, by email for support) will be
    used only to respond to your request and will be retained for as long as necessary to provide support or meet
    legal requirements.
  </p>

  <h2>6. Security</h2>
  <p>
    We do not operate online servers for IBG‑SGDgames titles covered by this policy. Local data stored on your
    device is protected by your device’s own security features (such as operating system permissions and user
    accounts).
  </p>
  <p>
    If you contact us by email, we take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of electronic
    communication is completely secure.
  </p>

  <h2>7. Your choices</h2>
  <p>
    You can:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Uninstall the game:</strong> This removes the game and its local data from your device.</li>
    <li><strong>Manage platform settings:</strong> If you obtained the game through a store or launcher, you can adjust that platform’s privacy and data settings.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>8. Changes to this policy</h2>
  <p>
    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published at the same URL where this
    policy is provided. The “Last updated” date at the top will indicate the latest revision.
  </p>
